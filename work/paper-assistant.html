Research Paper Assistant

Question answering over a library of computer-vision papers, with structure-aware chunking, hybrid retrieval, reranking and answers that cite their sources.

Role
Developer
Team
Solo
Status
Runs locally
Links
Code ↗

Problem

  • Papers break naive chunking: a fixed window cuts through equations, tables and figure captions, and the retrieved fragment then answers nothing.
  • For research questions, a plausible answer is worthless without the passage it came from.

What I built

  • PDF ingestion that follows document structure (sections, captions, references), so each chunk is a coherent piece of the paper.
  • Hybrid retrieval combining embeddings with keyword search, which keeps exact terms like model and dataset names findable.
  • Reranking on chunk metadata before the context is assembled, and answers returned with citations back to paper and section, served through FastAPI.

Key decisions

  • Hybrid over embeddings-only retrieval: model names and metrics are the queries that matter most, and they're lexical.
  • No answer without a citation. If retrieval comes back empty, the system says so.
